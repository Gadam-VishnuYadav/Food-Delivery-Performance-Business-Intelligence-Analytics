\# 🍔 Food Delivery Performance \& Business Intelligence Analytics



An end-to-end \*\*Power BI Business Intelligence project\*\* focused on analyzing food delivery operations, revenue, customer behavior, restaurant performance, promotions, and delivery efficiency.



The project transforms raw food delivery data into an interactive dashboard that provides business-focused insights for monitoring performance and identifying areas for improvement.

\# 🎯 Business Objective



The objective of this project is to build a centralized analytical dashboard that helps answer key business questions related to:



\- Revenue and order performance

\- Customer behavior and engagement

\- Restaurant performance

\- Food item and cuisine performance

\- Promotional campaign effectiveness

\- Order cancellations

\- Delivery efficiency

\- City-level operational performance

\# 💡 Business Questions



This dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:



\### Revenue

\- Which restaurants generate the highest revenue?

\- Which cuisines contribute most to revenue?

\- Which food items are the top revenue generators?

\- How much revenue is generated after considering discounts?



\### Customers

\- Which cities have the largest customer base?

\- How frequently do customers place orders?

\- Which customers contribute the highest spending?

\- Which payment methods are most commonly used?



\### Operations

\- Which cities have the highest delivery times?

\- Does delivery distance affect delivery time?

\- Where are cancelled orders concentrated?

\- How does customer rating vary across cities?



\### Promotions

\- Which promo codes generate the most orders?

\- Which promotions provide the highest average order value?

\- How much discount is given through promotional campaigns?
